# Certifications

## Cisco Networking Academy — Introduction to Cybersecurity

**Completed:** September 2026

### Course Overview

Cisco Networking Academy's Introduction to Cybersecurity provided a foundation in cybersecurity concepts, common threats, online safety, and the importance of protecting personal and organizational data.

### Topics and Concepts Studied

- Cybersecurity fundamentals
- Common cyber threats and attack methods
- Malware and malicious software
- Social engineering
- Data protection and privacy
- Network security concepts
- Denial-of-Service (DoS) and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks
- Internet of Things (IoT) security
- Malware examples and real-world attacks
- Cybersecurity career paths

### Real-World Examples Studied

During the course, I examined real-world cybersecurity incidents and malware examples, including:

- Stuxnet
- Zeus malware
- IoT-related attacks
- DoS and DDoS attacks

These examples helped connect cybersecurity concepts to actual incidents and demonstrate how different attack techniques can affect individuals, organizations, and infrastructure.

### What I Took Away

The course gave me a foundation for understanding how cyberattacks work and why layered security, user awareness, and defensive practices are important.

It also strengthened my interest in hands-on cybersecurity investigation and defensive security.

### Next Steps

I am continuing to build on this foundation through hands-on labs, networking studies, OSINT research, cybersecurity investigations, and additional coursework.
